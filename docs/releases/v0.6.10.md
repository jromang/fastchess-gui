A stopped tournament opened from the list resumes again whatever the size of its journal, and the window no longer
ends right after; in *Live*, the mosaic keeps its boards as a tournament's last games end; the overview's Elo chart
comes right under the standings.

## Resuming a tournament

- A stopped tournament opened from the list of tournaments, then resumed with *Resume*, could fail with "The
  tournament's process ended as it started" when its journal was large: the window still held the tournament's folder
  while it read it again, and the process that plays it gave up. The window now lets go of the folder before the
  process starts, and the process waits a few seconds for it.
- Right after such a resume, the window could end with "Error: std::bad_alloc". It no longer does.

## Live

- Each board of the mosaic keeps its last game, its final position, until the next game of its place starts there.
  As a tournament's last games end, the boards stay where they are instead of leaving one after the other, and the
  endings stay in sight.

## Overview

- The Elo chart comes right under the standings it draws, then *Search per engine*.
