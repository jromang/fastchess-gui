The tournaments list gives each tournament's time control, and Black's time bars can be read in the dark theme.

## Tournaments

- *All tournaments*, on the Tournaments page, has a *Time control* column, written as the tournament's header writes
  it: 40/60+0.6, 10+0.1, 1 s/move, nodes or depth.

## Graph

- In the dark theme, the bars of Black's moves in the time strip under a game's graph were almost black on the dark
  strip and could hardly be seen. They now have an outline, as White's bars have in the light theme.

Both reported on TalkChess by RanxXerox: thank you.
