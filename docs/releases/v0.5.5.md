PGN books as a participant's own book.

## Participants

- A participant's own book can now be a PGN book of the library, not only a Polyglot `.bin`. It is turned once into
  a Polyglot book: the moves of its games up to the chosen depth (*all*: their first 40 plies), each weighted by the
  number of games that play it. Games that cannot be read are left out and counted.
- The conversion runs in the background as soon as the book is chosen, with its progress in the *Book* cell, its
  popup and the summary. A start, a resume or the queue waits for it (*Waiting for the book of …*, with *Cancel*),
  and `--run` prepares it before the games, printing its progress.
- The result is kept in the profile (`book-cache`) and made again only when the PGN file, the depth or the variant
  changes; books unused for 30 days are removed, never one a running tournament uses.
- A tournament plays from the book prepared when it was started or resumed, even if the PGN file is saved again
  meanwhile. A withdrawn or replaced participant's book no longer holds a resume.
- A book that cannot be prepared shows why in its popup, with *Try again*; each start also tries again.
- EPD books stay greyed: they hold positions, not moves.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `fastchess-gui-v0.5.5-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | `fastchess-gui` and the `fastchess` command line tool, x86-64 Linux (glibc 2.35 or newer). Needs X11 or Wayland and OpenGL 3.2. |
| `fastchess-gui-v0.5.5-windows-x86_64.zip` | `fastchess-gui.exe` and `fastchess.exe`, 64-bit Windows 10 version 1903 or newer. No installer, nothing else to install. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums of the two archives. |

The binaries are built by this repository's GitHub Actions workflow; the tests run on Linux and on Windows.
