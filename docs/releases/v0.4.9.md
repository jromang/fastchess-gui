Three more fixes for engine lists imported from other programs.

## Fixes

- **An engine that answers from its own book passes the UCI compliance check** (#45). With no search line to verify
  after a book move (Amyan 1.72 writes only "info string  e2e4 70%,d2d4 30%"), the check counted a failure.
- **An engine whose options could not be read has them read again after its next check.** Earlier versions refused
  some engines (Cerebro 3.03d, Amyan 1.72); their options stayed empty until "Read the UCI options again".
- **Only the engines scroll in the Engines list**: Add, Import, Duplicate, Remove and the search stay in sight, the
  legend too.
