Opening books, engine checks, ratings across tournaments and fair play.

## Changes

**Engines**
- A **quick test** on each engine: 10 games at 10+0.1 from 5 positions, against itself or another engine, played
  like a real match. Its result stays with the engine: score, losses on time, crashes, illegal moves, and the share
  of its clock it used.
- A **missing or rebuilt program** is noticed: a missing one is marked with *Locate…* and a tournament does not
  start with it; a rebuilt one has its options read again. Tournaments record the programs they were started with
  and warn when one changed since.
- **Engine logs**: every line exchanged with both engines is kept for the games lost on time, by a crash, a stall
  or an illegal move (or every game, if asked), in `logs/` of the tournament folder, opened from the Games tab.
- **Fair play**: the processor time of every engine is measured while it thinks and while its opponent does; the
  Overview warns when an engine uses more cores than its threads, or thinks on its opponent's time.

**Opening books**
- A **Books** page: a library of books (EPD, PGN, Polyglot), each checked for illegal positions, duplicates,
  positions with colours reversed and games already over, browsed on a board, and saved as a cleaned copy. The
  setup offers them by name.
- **Polyglot** `.bin` books, followed to a depth and played as positions.
- An **Openings** tab in each tournament: results by starting position, with the openings that favour a colour,
  those always drawn and those that tell the engines apart; exported as EPD.

**Games**
- The **opening** of every game (ECO code and name) in the Games tab, and a filter by code, range or name.
- **Interesting games**: filters for the games where the engines disagree, where an evaluation swings, or that
  were lost from a winning score, each opened at that moment.

**Ratings**
- Four tabs: the **rating list** (minimum number of games, an export for Ordo and BayesElo), an **engine's card**
  (results by time control, losses on time, crashes and illegal moves, its tournaments, search statistics, its
  rating over time), **head to head** records, and the **progress** of an engine's versions.
- Filters by time control, group of the engine library and period, and games of **imported PGN files** (CCRL,
  CEGT, other programs).

**Sharing**
- *Copy* as text or as **BBCode** for a forum post: standings, crosstable, match result, rating list.

## Known limitations

- The engines of imported PGN files are known by the names the files give them; they are not matched to the
  engine library.
- Engine logs hold what the engines write on their standard output, not their error output.
- Processor use is measured while games are played and starts again when a tournament is resumed.
- WinBoard engines: no option for engines that report scores from White's point of view, and engines asking to be
  restarted for every game (`reuse=0`) are not (tick *Restart the engine for every game*). An engine of protocol 1
  without `setboard` nor edit mode can only start from the initial position.
- The TLCS server has been checked against node-tlcv's documentation and source and a client written from it,
  not yet against TLCV itself or a running node-tlcv.
- On Wayland, detached boards stay inside the main window.
