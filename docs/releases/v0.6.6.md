An engine added to a running tournament takes the tournament's settings, or settings of its own; the analysis engine can be unloaded.

## Tournaments

- An engine added or brought in while a tournament runs (*Add an engine*, *Replace an engine*) took the time control,
  Hash and Threads of the tournament's first engine, its time odds and settings of its own included. It now takes the
  tournament's, shown under the engine, or *Settings of its own*: Hash, Threads, share of the time (or nodes, depth),
  as a participant's own settings on the setup page. A tournament records its settings from this version on; in one
  made before, those of its first engine still stand in for them.
- The tournament's header, the time control the *Ratings* page files its games under, and the forum and footer lines
  of the standings give the tournament's time control, no longer the first engine's with its time odds.

## Board

- *Unload the engine*, a power button beside the analysis's engine (also in the command palette), ends the analysis
  engine and the threat's: once stopped, they stayed started with their hash table, and a network engine's GPU
  memory, until the program quit. *Analyse* starts the engine again.
- The analysis's row goes on a second line where the card is narrow, rather than drawing the threads over the
  settings.

The binaries are built by this repository's GitHub Actions workflow; the tests run on Linux, Windows and macOS. The
archives carry the licenses of the libraries built in, of the piece sets and of the 3D board's pieces and tone mapping
(see `ATTRIBUTION.md`).
