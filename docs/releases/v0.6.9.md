In *Live*, the engines' cards keep their height through a game; the overview's Elo chart is there whatever the number
of engines; the engines' logos are now optional.

## Live and the big screen

- The engines' cards no longer grow and shrink with each line: a card keeps, until the end of the game, the most room
  its line has needed (within the room left above the moves) and the most its other parts have taken, so that the
  move list and the evaluation curve under the cards stay in place. Beside the mosaic, the short lines keep their
  height the same way; on the big screen each line takes two rows whatever its length, so that the tablebases' verdict
  under the box no longer moves.
- In a window too low for the evaluation curve, a line says it is left out, as the *Games* tab now does for its charts.

## Overview

- The Elo chart (the dots with their 95 % intervals) is always shown, at least as high as its engines need: with many
  engines, the column scrolls down to it under the standings and the search per engine. Since 0.6.4 it was left out,
  without a word, past about 16 engines in a window of ordinary height.

## Appearance

- The engines' logos in *Live* and on the big screen are optional and off by default: *Settings > Appearance > Logos*,
  also in Ctrl+K. An engine's card keeps the choice of its logo and says when the logos are off.
