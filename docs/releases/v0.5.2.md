Tournament panels that dock where you want them, with ready-made and saved layouts.

## Panels and layouts

- Every tab of a tournament (Overview, Standings, Bracket, Schedule, Broadcast, Live, Games, Openings) is a panel.
  By default they are tabs, as before. Drag one by its tab to dock it beside, above or below another, or to float
  it, also onto a second screen on Windows and Linux/X11 (not on Wayland).
- The game followed in *Live* comes apart in *Board*, *Players*, *Searches*, *Moves* and *Chart* panels, and
  detached boards dock like the other panels.
- The *Layout* button offers ready-made layouts: *Tabs*, *Follow a game* (the board on the left, the players,
  searches and chart on the right, the standings under them), *Large tournament* (the mosaic beside the
  standings) and *Broadcast*. *Save the layout as…* keeps your own under a name, *Delete* removes one, *Default
  layout* goes back to tabs, and *Panels* shows or hides each panel.
- The command palette (Ctrl+K) lists every panel and every layout.
- The arrangement is kept between sessions, even with every panel floating. The first launch of this version shows
  the default layout once.
- A saved layout opens with its floating panels where they were saved. Parts for panels a format lacks (a bracket
  outside a knockout, standings in a match) are left out rather than shown as an empty pane.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `fastchess-gui-v0.5.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | `fastchess-gui` and the `fastchess` command line tool, x86-64 Linux (glibc 2.35 or newer). Needs X11 or Wayland and OpenGL 3.2. |
| `fastchess-gui-v0.5.2-windows-x86_64.zip` | `fastchess-gui.exe` and `fastchess.exe`, 64-bit Windows 10 version 1903 or newer. No installer, nothing else to install. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums of the two archives. |

The binaries are built by this repository's GitHub Actions workflow; the tests run on Linux and on Windows.
