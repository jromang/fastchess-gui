Play a game against an engine on the Board page, a piece moved by two clicks or dragged, in 2D and in 3D; Clone a
tournament; and each engine's search, its NPS among it, in the overview of a tournament.

## Play against an engine

- *Play a game…* on the Board page lists Human first among White and Black: that side is yours, your moves made on the
  board with the mouse, on the flat board as on the 3D one.
- The board turns to your side at each game and takes your pieces only at your turn (*Your move* in the card); a click
  while looking back brings it to the game. Your clock runs as an engine's, a loss on time included.
- *Resign the game* ends it at any moment, and the second game of a pair follows.
- While you play, the engine's scores, depths and lines stay out of the game shown: no evaluation bar, no curve,
  nothing under *This move*. The finished game has them.
- Only the engine counts in the core budget; the PGN names you by your login name.

## Drag and drop

- A piece can be dragged to its square on the Board page, as well as played by a click on it and one on its square:
  in analysis, in variations and in a game against an engine alike. The square it would land on is tinted; let go
  where it cannot go, it goes back, still picked up.
- On the 3D board a translucent ghost of the piece follows the pointer, floating above the board and passing behind
  the pieces in front of it; the piece moves when it is let go.
- The 3D board picks a piece by its silhouette, no longer by a cylinder as wide as its widest part: a click on the
  lower part of a pawn behind a back-rank piece picks the pawn.

## Tournaments

- *Clone*, in a tournament's header, under *Last finished* and in *All tournaments* on the home page, and in the
  command palette, opens the New tournament page filled with that tournament's setup, whatever its format: the
  participants with their own settings, the time control, the openings, the adjudication and the rest. Each
  tournament now keeps its setup in its folder (`setup.json`) when it starts; one started before this version cannot
  be cloned.
- *Relaunch with…* plays a match again with its own settings when its folder kept them, rather than with those of the
  New tournament page.
- The overview of a tournament has *Search per engine* under the standings, as a match had: each engine's average
  depth, NPS, time per move, decision time and processor use, book moves left out, sorted by a click on a column.
  Beside the NPS, ×4 says the engine searches with 4 threads. A match's table sorts the same way, and shows — for what
  an engine does not tell.

## Live view

- The mosaic shows as many boards as games being played, no longer one for each game the concurrency allows: a
  tournament with a concurrency of 32 and four games left showed 32 small boards, 28 of them empty. The game followed
  moves to a board that has one.
- When the boards would need a scroll bar, the mosaic takes width from the game in focus, down to 40 % of the page,
  before scrolling.
