A 3D board for the Board page, an option, lit as a photographer lights a still life; and the analysis's arrows can
now be turned off.

## The 3D board

- Settings > Appearance > *3D board*: the Board page's board drawn in three dimensions, off by default. Seven
  lightings (*Studio*, *Low key*, *High key*, *Showroom*, *Window light*, *Golden hour*, *Tournament hall*), the
  exposure, the board's varnish and its gloss, and the quality; the preview of Appearance shows the 3D board while it
  is on.
- It is path-traced by the graphics card: the picture refines for a few seconds while nothing moves, a few
  milliseconds of the card each frame, then the card rests. Fewer samples (*Quality*) spare a processor's graphics
  during tournaments. While the board turns, a preview at a lower resolution.
- What the 2D board shows is there: the eval bar, the last move, the piece picked up and where it may go, a king in
  check, the analysis's arrows, which show faintly through the pieces.
- A left click picks a square as on the 2D board, on a piece or on the board behind it; the right button held turns
  round the board (a right click still lets the piece go), the wheel comes closer, a click of the wheel brings the
  view back; F, or *Flip*, turns the camera half way round.
- It needs OpenGL 3.3. Where the window is drawn without it (the software renderer, some virtual machines), the boards
  stay flat and Appearance says why. The program grows by about 300 KB.
- The pieces are clarkerubber's Staunton-Pieces, the tone mapping Benjamin Wrensch's minimal implementation of Troy
  Sobotka's AgX, both under the MIT license (see `ATTRIBUTION.md`).

## Other changes

- The Board page's analysis has an *Arrows* checkbox: without the arrows, in 2D as in 3D, the lines below still show
  the moves.
- The window asks the graphics driver for OpenGL 3.3 first, then 3.2 as before.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `fastchess-gui-v0.6.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | `fastchess-gui`, x86-64 Linux (glibc 2.35 or newer). Needs X11 or Wayland. |
| `fastchess-gui-v0.6.2-windows-x86_64.zip` | `fastchess-gui.exe`, 64-bit Windows 10 version 1903 or newer. No installer, nothing else to install. |
| `fastchess-gui-v0.6.2-macos-universal.zip` | `fastchess-gui.app` and the `fastchess` command line tool, macOS 13.3 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel. Not signed: Control-click it and choose Open the first time. |

The binaries are built by this repository's GitHub Actions workflow; the tests run on Linux, Windows and macOS. The
archives carry the licenses of the libraries built in, of the piece sets and of the 3D board's pieces and tone mapping
(see `ATTRIBUTION.md`).
